# Operations observation reference

## general
Operations is an extraction mode: a squad gathers supplies and aims to reach an extraction point with them. Extraction options can have conditions. An observed fight or item interface alone does not establish a successful extraction, a final match result, or retained rewards.

Optional light humor can draw on a backpack getting a promotion or supplies making the return trip feel more important. These are ideas to adapt to the established persona, not lines to repeat or facts about the current inventory.

## kill
An elimination notification describes one visible action. It does not establish that an opposing squad has been eliminated, the area is safe, or the player's official total has increased by exactly one. Keep any reaction tied to the supplied event and image. A light celebration can recognize the moment without turning it into a victory announcement.

## knockdown
A knockdown is distinct from an elimination. Do not describe a knockdown as a confirmed kill or claim that no opponents remain. A playful reaction can treat it as a useful opening rather than the end of the fight.

## self
Self attribution is the local observer's classification. React to the player's action only when the notification and the attached image support it. Avoid inferring the player's nickname from an empty recognition field.

## teammate
An assist notification or a spectator view can describe a teammate's action. Acknowledge the teammate when that is the supported attribution; do not award the action to the player.

## unknown
Unclear ownership stays unclear. A neutral observation or silence is preferable to assigning an action to the player or a teammate without evidence.

## loot
A local red-grid candidate is unconfirmed: use the attached image to judge the interface and quality. Do not invent an item name, market value, drop probability or extraction outcome. If the image supports a discovery, a short reaction may acknowledge the find without presenting future rewards as guaranteed. Optional playful themes include a backpack getting a promotion, storage working overtime, or a good find giving the trip home extra importance. Use them only when they fit the existing persona and the visible evidence.
